# Week 4 Project Report — Key Findings

## Dataset overview
- Records: 1,200 orders
- Fields: 14
- Date range: January 2023 to June 2025
- Unique customers: 1,189
- Total revenue: $1,264,761.96
- Average order value: $1,053.97

## Key observations
1. Chair generated the highest total revenue among products: $195,620.11.
2. Printer was very close to Chair at $195,612.61.
3. Credit Card generated the highest revenue among payment methods: $263,847.63.
4. Instagram was the highest-revenue referral source: $275,285.45.
5. Cancelled orders represented 250 of the 1,200 orders (20.8%).
6. FREESHIP was the largest coupon group by revenue at $335,036.99.
7. The dashboard allows users to filter the analysis by date, product, order status, payment method, and referral source.

## Storytelling approach
The dashboard starts with KPIs, then moves from time trend to product performance, operational status, acquisition source, payment behavior, coupon performance, and finally executive takeaways. This follows the Week 4 principle of moving from pattern discovery toward decision-oriented communication.
